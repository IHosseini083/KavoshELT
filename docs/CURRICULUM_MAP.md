# Iranian Secondary English Curriculum Map (Grades 7–12)

Detailed roadmap of all 31 official lessons, their thematic scopes, target grammatical structures, and assessment guidelines according to the Iranian Ministry of Education (*Daftar-e-Talif*).

---

## Junior High School (Lower Secondary) — *Prospect Series*

### Grade 7 (Prospect 1) — 8 Lessons
1. **Lesson 1: My Name**  
   - *Grammar:* To be (am, is, are), What questions, My/Your possessive adjectives.  
   - *Theme:* Greetings, introduction, alphabet spelling.
2. **Lesson 2: My Classmates**  
   - *Grammar:* Who questions, demonstratives (this, that, these, those).  
   - *Theme:* Friends, classmates, school environment.
3. **Lesson 3: My Age**  
   - *Grammar:* How old questions, numbers 1–100, Persian calendar months.  
   - *Theme:* Age, birthdays, numbers.
4. **Lesson 4: My Family**  
   - *Grammar:* What is his/her job, family relations.  
   - *Theme:* Family tree, professions, daily work.
5. **Lesson 5: My Appearance**  
   - *Grammar:* Descriptive adjectives, colors, clothing nouns.  
   - *Theme:* Physical appearance, height, hair, clothing.
6. **Lesson 6: My House**  
   - *Grammar:* Present Continuous (doing household actions), there is / there are.  
   - *Theme:* Rooms, furniture, household routines.
7. **Lesson 7: My Address**  
   - *Grammar:* Telling time, days of the week, prepositions of time and place (at, on).  
   - *Theme:* Home address, phone numbers, schedule.
8. **Lesson 8: My Favorite Food**  
   - *Grammar:* Expressing preferences (like, want), polite requests.  
   - *Theme:* Food, traditional drinks, Iranian meals.

### Grade 8 (Prospect 2) — 7 Lessons
1. **Lesson 1: My Nationality** — Where are you from?, nationalities, world countries.
2. **Lesson 2: My Week** — Simple Present (do/does), weekly schedules, daily routines.
3. **Lesson 3: My Abilities** — Modal *can / can't*, asking about talents and sports.
4. **Lesson 4: My Health** — Health problems (headache, fever, sore throat), advice.
5. **Lesson 5: My City** — City facilities, weather adjectives, geographical locations.
6. **Lesson 6: My Village** — Farming, livestock, rural life, nature.
7. **Lesson 7: My Hobbies** — Free time activities, sports, reading books.

### Grade 9 (Prospect 3) — 6 Lessons
1. **Lesson 1: Personality** — Personality traits (talkative, quiet, patient, brave, messy).
2. **Lesson 2: Travel** — Booking hotel, train station, airport, present continuous for near future.
3. **Lesson 3: Festivals and Ceremonies** — Nowruz, Yalda, Eid-e-Fitr, cultural rituals.
4. **Lesson 4: Services** — Public services (bank, post office, fire station, police).
5. **Lesson 5: Media** — Simple Past tense (regular -ed and irregular verbs), did questions.
6. **Lesson 6: Health and Injuries** — Accidents, emergency, treatments, simple past narratives.

## Senior High School (Upper Secondary) — *Vision Series*

### Grade 10 (Vision 1) — 4 Lessons
1. **Lesson 1: Saving Nature**  
   - *Grammar:* Simple Future with *will / won't*, *be going to*, singular/plural nouns.  
   - *Theme:* Endangered wildlife, Iranian cheetah, protecting natural habitats.
2. **Lesson 2: Wonders of Creation**  
   - *Grammar:* Adjectives (comparatives, superlatives, order of adjectives).  
   - *Theme:* Human heart, blood cells, astronomy, planets, telescopes.
3. **Lesson 3: The Value of Knowledge**  
   - *Grammar:* Past Progressive (*was/were + ing*), when/while clauses, self-pronouns.  
   - *Theme:* Dr. Mohammad Gharib, Louis Pasteur, laboratory experiments.
4. **Lesson 4: Traveling the World**  
   - *Grammar:* Modals (*can, may, must, should*), prepositions.  
   - *Theme:* Iranian tourism, Shiraz, Persepolis, Isfahan, handicrafts.

### Grade 11 (Vision 2) — 3 Lessons
1. **Lesson 1: Understanding People**  
   - *Grammar:* Present Perfect tense (*have/has + p.p.*), *for* and *since*, count/noncount nouns.  
   - *Theme:* Human languages, communication, deaf culture, sign language.
2. **Lesson 2: A Healthy Lifestyle**  
   - *Grammar:* Gerunds and Infinitives, Conditional Sentences Type 1.  
   - *Theme:* Nutrition, balanced diet, exercise, stress prevention.
3. **Lesson 3: Art and Culture**  
   - *Grammar:* Conditional Sentences Type 1, Infinitives of purpose.  
   - *Theme:* Persian art, calligraphy, pottery, carpets, cultural heritage.

### Grade 12 (Vision 3) — 3 Lessons
1. **Lesson 1: Sense of Appreciation**  
   - *Grammar:* Passive Voice (Present and Past Simple), Tag Questions, Compound Sentences (*and, but, or, so*).  
   - *Theme:* Honoring elders, nursing homes, family gratitude.
2. **Lesson 2: Look it Up!**  
   - *Grammar:* Passive Voice with modals, Relative Clauses (*who, which, that*), Conditional Sentences Type 2.  
   - *Theme:* Dictionaries, reference works, vocabulary strategies.
3. **Lesson 3: Renewable Energy**  
   - *Grammar:* Past Perfect tense (*had + p.p.*), Passive Voice across all tenses.  
   - *Theme:* Clean energy, solar power, wind turbines, environmental protection.
